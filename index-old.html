AI Validating Technique

Learn nine practical techniques to constrain Large Language Models, reduce hallucination, eliminate inaccuracy, and secure reliable enterprise outputs.

Rationale for the Workshop

As Generative AI becomes embedded into professional workflows, its major liability remains hallucination—the plausible-sounding fabrication of data. Relying on unchecked AI draft material introduces profound cybersecurity vulnerabilities, academic policy errors, compliance exposure, and operational risks.

This workshop equips leaders, educators, and technicians with programmatic prompts and design workflows that ground AI in verified documents, cross-check parameters using multiple model sources, and preserve high standards of accuracy through systematic feedback mechanisms.

Focus Areas: College Policies, Cybersecurity Standards, Compliance Reports
